# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Welcome different backgrounds and
experience levels. Discuss technical disagreements on evidence. Do not harass,
threaten, discriminate, share private information or use sexualized language.
These expectations apply in project discussions, reviews and community spaces.

Maintainers may edit/remove abusive content, warn participants or restrict access.
Responses should be proportional and documented privately. Report concerns
privately to Brandon Musa. To request a private reporting channel, open a GitHub
issue asking for contact details without describing the incident. A dedicated
reporting address and independent appeal contact are not currently available. A conflicted
maintainer must recuse themselves from handling a report.
